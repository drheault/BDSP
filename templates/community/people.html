How ratings work

You rate yourself on each skill (Aware → Recognised authority). Colleagues endorse you with a level and must say where they saw the skill in action. One endorsement per person per skill, never on yourself. The combined level is the average of your rating and all endorsements, so a self-rating alone never outranks peers.
