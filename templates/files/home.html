What “sync” does

Indexing a project folder lists every file under it and creates a record per file (site visit reports, photos, monitoring, progress) based on the sub-folder name, without copying the data. The files stay on the share; the app keeps the path, date and type so they can be searched, tagged and pulled into proposals and estimates.
